# Pneumonia Overview

## What Pneumonia Is

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs (alveoli) in one or both lungs.
The alveoli may fill with fluid or pus, causing cough with phlegm, fever, chills, and
difficulty breathing. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi, with bacterial
and viral pneumonia being the most common types in adults.

## Who Is at Higher Risk

- Infants and young children
- Adults over 65
- People with chronic conditions (asthma, COPD, diabetes, heart disease)
- People with weakened immune systems
- Smokers
- Hospitalized or recently hospitalized patients (a distinct category, healthcare-associated pneumonia)

## Key Takeaway

Chest imaging findings suggestive of pneumonia are supportive evidence, not a
standalone diagnosis. Correlation with clinical symptoms, physical examination, and
often laboratory testing is required for a confirmed diagnosis and to guide treatment.
