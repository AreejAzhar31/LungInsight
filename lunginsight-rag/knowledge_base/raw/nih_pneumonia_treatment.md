# Pneumonia Treatment Overview

## General Approach

Treatment depends on the suspected cause (bacterial, viral, or fungal), the patient's
age and overall health, and the severity of illness.

- **Bacterial pneumonia** is typically treated with antibiotics, selected based on
  local resistance patterns, patient history, and severity.
- **Viral pneumonia** treatment focuses on supportive care (rest, fluids, fever and
  symptom management); antiviral medication may be used for specific viral causes
  when appropriate and started early.
- **Fungal pneumonia** is less common and treated with antifungal medication, usually
  in patients with weakened immune systems.

## Supportive Care

Regardless of cause, supportive measures often include:

- Rest and adequate fluid intake
- Fever and pain management
- Supplemental oxygen if blood oxygen levels are low
- Monitoring for worsening symptoms or complications

## When Hospitalization Is Needed

Hospitalization may be recommended for patients with:

- Low blood oxygen levels requiring supplemental oxygen
- Signs of severe illness (confusion, low blood pressure, rapid breathing)
- Significant underlying health conditions
- Inability to maintain adequate fluid intake or tolerate oral medication

## Important Caveat

Treatment decisions require an individualized clinical assessment by a qualified
healthcare provider. This information is general and does not constitute treatment
guidance for any specific patient.
